At Vivan Interiors, we believe a home should be more than visually beautiful—it should feel effortless, personal, and distinctly yours.

Founded by Prabha Agarwal and Rohit Agarwal, Vivan Interiors is a residential interior design studio focused on creating refined, functional and timeless spaces for modern Indian homes.

02  /  OUR DESIGN PHILOSOPHYLESS NOISE. MORE INTENTION.

Less noise.More intention.

Modern, elegant and enduring.
Never designed just for the moment.

We are drawn towards interiors that feel modern, elegant and enduring rather than designs that simply follow short-lived trends. Our spaces combine clean forms, thoughtful lighting, carefully selected materials, practical storage and subtle detailing.

Luxury does not always mean extravagance. It can be found in proportion, craftsmanship, comfort and attention to detail. Good design should respect the investment behind it—with budget, usability and long-term value considered from the beginning.

03OUR APPROACH

Designing homes,
not just interiors.

Every home has its own character. Every family, its own way of living.

Our approach begins by understanding the people behind the space—their routines, preferences, aspirations and practical requirements. From the initial layout to the smallest finishing detail, every decision is made with a clear purpose.

We carefully balance aesthetics, functionality, comfort and budget, so the final design feels cohesive without becoming impractical or excessive.

Apartment, bungalow, private villa or thoughtfully planned office—the aim stays the same: to create spaces that feel considered, comfortable and uniquely yours.
