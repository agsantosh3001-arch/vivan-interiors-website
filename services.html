THE SPACES01 — 06

Homes shaped around their people, and rooms shaped around real life.

01Residential
interiors

Homes designed around the people who live in them.

A home is perhaps the most personal space we design. Every family has different routines, priorities and preferences. We begin by understanding those differences, then bring planning, proportions, materials, lighting, furniture and everyday functionality together across apartments, premium residences, bungalows and villas.

Our intention is never to make one home look like another. Every home should have its own identity.

  • Complete home interiors
  • Apartments & premium residences
  • Bungalows, villas & duplex homes
  • Individual room interiors
  • Home renovations
  • Custom furniture & storage
02Living
spaces

Where the home comes together.

The living room is where everyday life happens. We plan around conversation, movement, comfort and visual balance—considering furniture, lighting, walls, ceilings and storage as one composition. Proportion matters: a room does not become better simply because more elements are added.

Sometimes, good design is knowing what to leave out.

  • TV & entertainment units
  • Feature walls & wall panelling
  • Custom seating layouts
  • Display units & storage
  • False ceilings
  • Ambient & decorative lighting
  • Partitions
  • Custom furniture
03Quiet
bedrooms

Personal, calm and comfortable.

A bedroom should not feel like a showroom. It should feel like your space. From the placement of the bed to the wardrobe, dressing area, bedside lighting and materials, every decision is made to create a room that feels calm and effortless.

A bedroom should make you want to come home to it.

  • Master, children's & guest bedrooms
  • Wardrobes & dressing areas
  • Headboard walls
  • Bedside units
  • Study corners
  • Custom storage
  • Ambient lighting
04Modular
kitchens

Designed for real life.

A kitchen has to work before it has to look beautiful. We first ask how it will be used, what needs to be stored, who uses it, where appliances belong and how movement can be made easier. Then we build the visual design around the answers.

A good kitchen makes everyday work simpler, more organised and more enjoyable.

  • Modular base & wall units
  • Tall units & pantry solutions
  • Kitchen islands & breakfast counters
  • Appliance integration
  • Crockery storage
  • Hardware selection
  • Countertop planning
  • Lighting, materials & finishes
05Wardrobes
& storage

Storage that belongs to the design.

A wardrobe or storage unit should not simply occupy a wall. We plan it around what you need to store, how often you use it and how the space around it functions.

The best storage is often the storage you hardly notice.

  • Hinged & sliding wardrobes
  • Full-height & walk-in wardrobes
  • Dressing units
  • Shoe & loft storage
  • Crockery units
  • Display cabinets
  • Utility storage
  • Custom built-in furniture
06Lighting
& ceilings

Light is part of the architecture.

Lighting changes the way a room looks, feels and functions. We plan it alongside the interior—not as an afterthought—bringing natural, task, ambient and decorative light into a considered balance.

A well-lit home should feel warm and natural—not simply bright.

  • False ceiling design
  • Cove & recessed lighting
  • Pendant & decorative fixtures
  • Accent & wall lighting
  • Feature lighting
  • Lighting layouts
THE DESIGN LANGUAGE07 — 10

Details with a purpose: planned for your space, your eye and your everyday.

07Custom
furniture

Made for your space.

Ready-made furniture does not always fit the space or the requirement. We design pieces around exact dimensions, proportions and purpose—from entertainment units and study tables to wardrobes, storage systems and distinctive furniture.

Dimensions. Materials. Function. Proportion. Finish. Furniture that feels as though it was always meant to be part of the room.

08Materials
& finishes

The details that define the space.

We consider materials for their appearance, suitability, durability, maintenance and relationship with the rest of the home. Each project gets a palette that feels considered and cohesive.

Not everything needs to stand out. Some things simply need to work together.

  • Laminates & veneers
  • Wood finishes & paints
  • Wallpapers
  • Glass & metals
  • Tiles & stone
  • Countertop materials
  • Hardware & fabrics
  • Decorative finishes
092D space
planning

Before beauty comes planning.

One of the most important parts of an interior is often something you never see: the planning behind it. Our 2D layouts establish the foundation, studying dimensions, circulation, furniture placement, storage, electrical points and functional relationships.

Good planning reduces confusion later. A space should work on paper before it is built in reality.

  • Furniture & space planning
  • Kitchen layouts
  • Storage planning
  • Electrical layouts
  • Ceiling layouts
  • Lighting positions
  • Flooring layouts
  • Detailed measurements
103D design
& visualization

See the design before we build it.

Detailed 3D visualization brings a proposed design to life before execution. It helps you understand how furniture, colours, materials, lighting, ceilings, wall finishes, storage, proportions and atmosphere relate to one another—so decisions can be refined before site work begins.

For us, 3D is not about an impressive picture. It is about clarity between what is imagined and what is eventually built.

THE BIGGER PICTURE11 — 14

From a single room to an entire property, from the first plan to the final finish.

11Renovation
& redesign

When an existing space needs a new direction.

Not every home needs to be completely rebuilt. We identify what already works, what does not, and redesign intelligently. We approach existing spaces with respect: the objective is not to remove everything because it is old, but to make the space work better for the life being lived in it today.

  • Space replanning
  • Furniture replacement
  • Storage upgrades
  • Kitchen renovation
  • Bedroom redesign
  • Lighting & ceiling improvements
  • Wall treatments & flooring
  • Complete interior refurbishment
12Bungalows
& villas

One property. One connected home.

Larger residences call for a different level of planning. Multiple rooms, floors, circulation areas and family requirements come together without making the home feel disconnected. We create continuity across the property while allowing individual spaces to retain their own character.

  • Living & family lounges
  • Bedrooms & dining areas
  • Kitchens & staircases
  • Pooja rooms
  • Home offices
  • Entertainment spaces
  • Walk-in wardrobes
  • Custom furniture & storage
13Office &
corporate

Professional spaces with purpose.

An office should represent the organisation while working efficiently for the people inside it. We shape environments that feel professional without becoming impersonal.

  • Reception & waiting areas
  • Workstations
  • Meeting rooms
  • Director cabins
  • Storage & pantry spaces
  • Lighting & ceiling design
  • Furniture planning
  • Brand integration
14Interior
execution

Because the drawing is only the beginning.

A thoughtful design can lose its character through poor execution, inconsistent materials or careless finishing. We coordinate with site teams and skilled craftsmen to translate the approved design into the actual space with accuracy and care.

  • Carpentry
  • Electrical & plumbing work
  • Painting & ceiling work
  • Flooring
  • Modular installations
  • Furniture & lighting
  • Glass & metal work
  • Finishing

The final result should look like the design—not merely resemble it.
